Evaluation knowledge should compound, not scatter.

Methods, code, and results are spread across papers, repositories, and posts, and much of the underlying data is never released. The commons is built on three commitments.

Open by default

Everything we collect and host is freely accessible and reusable, so evaluation claims can be checked by anyone.

Granular and traceable

We favor evidence at the level it is produced, down to individual items and responses, with links back to every source.

Built together

The commons grows through community contributions and stays useful because many people maintain it.

The commons is only as good as what we bring to it.
