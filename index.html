Goals for the science of AI evaluation

A science of AI evaluation needs more than more benchmarks.

Centered on people

Start from the people affected and the decisions they face, and build evaluations that provide evidence for what matters to them.

Evaluate the evaluations

Benchmarks and metrics are measurement instruments. Test their reliability and validity, and develop measurement models designed for AI evaluation.

From research to practice

Practitioners, auditors, policymakers, and the public need evaluations they can understand, trust, and act on.

The commons is only as good as what we bring to it
